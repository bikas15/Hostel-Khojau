# Hostel Hub - Features Overview

Welcome to Hostel Hub! Your complete solution for finding and booking hostels with ease. Here's everything our platform offers:

## 🏠 For Travelers & Students

### **Browse & Discover Hostels**
- **Search by Location**: Find hostels in your preferred city or area
- **Interactive Map**: See all hostels on a map with exact locations
- **Photo Galleries**: View high-quality photos of rooms and common areas
- **Filter Options**: Sort by price, amenities, room types, and ratings

### **Easy Booking Process**
- **Room Selection**: Choose from Single, Double, Triple, or Dorm rooms
- **Flexible Stays**: Book for 1 month or longer periods
- **Instant Availability**: See real-time room availability
- **Secure Payments**: Safe and secure payment processing with Khalti
- **Booking Confirmation**: Get instant confirmation after payment

### **Manage Your Bookings**
- **My Bookings**: View all your current and past bookings in one place
- **Booking Details**: See check-in dates, room info, and total costs
- **Cancel Bookings**: Cancel bookings if plans change
- **Delete Records**: Remove cancelled booking records to keep your list clean
- **Booking History**: Track all your hostel stays over time

### **Communication & Reviews**
- **Direct Chat**: Message hostel owners directly with questions
- **AI Assistant**: Get instant help from our smart chatbot 24/7
- **Write Reviews**: Share your experience and help other travelers
- **Read Reviews**: See what others say before booking
- **Rating System**: Rate hostels from 1 to 5 stars
